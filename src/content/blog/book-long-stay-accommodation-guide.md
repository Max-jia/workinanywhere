I have lost a deposit to a fake listing. I have worked from a kitchen stool that was advertised as a "dedicated workspace." I once spent two weeks in an apartment with WiFi so bad I could not load Gmail. Finding a good apartment for a month or longer is the most expensive part of this lifestyle when it goes wrong.

This is what I have learned about booking long stays, avoiding scams, and saving money compared to Airbnb.

---

## The platform landscape

### Fee-free platforms (save 14–16% vs Airbnb)

| Platform | Best For | Guest Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Houfy | Direct booking, all regions | $0 (no guest or host fees) |
| Furnished Finder | 30+ day stays, US-focused | $0 guest fee |
| Flatio | Europe, verified lease contracts | No hidden fees |
| Nomad Stays | Verified WiFi and workspace | Low/no guest fee |

Houfy has 97,000+ listings in 50+ countries. It is a marketplace that connects guests directly with hosts and charges neither side. You save $140 to $200+ per booking compared to the same listing on Airbnb.

Furnished Finder was built for traveling nurses but has become popular with remote workers. It has 300,000+ listings, direct landlord communication, and no guest fees. Best for 30+ day stays in the US.

### Nomad-specific platforms

| Platform | Focus |
|---|---|
| Nomad Stays | Independently verifies WiFi speeds and requires dedicated workspaces |
| Roampads | Certifies home offices: ergonomic chairs, proper desks, verified internet |
| Coliving.com | Global coliving marketplace for community-focused stays |
| Outsite | Premium co-living with high speed internet, consistent quality across locations |
| RemoteBase | Budget-friendly curated long stay options |

These platforms treat WiFi and a desk as core infrastructure, not optional extras. I have used Nomad Stays twice and their verified WiFi speeds were accurate both times.

### Other worthwhile alternatives

- Kindred and HomeExchange: House swapping. $15 to $35/night service fee or $220/year membership. Over 200,000 members. Best if you have a home to swap.
- TrustedHousesitters: Free accommodation in exchange for pet or house sitting. Works well if you are flexible about location.
- Blueground: Professionally managed furnished apartments in 50+ cities. Higher cost, consistent quality.
- VRBO: Strong for whole home family rentals but charges similar fees to Airbnb.

## The scam prevention checklist

| Red Flag | Safe Practice |
|---|---|
| Price far below market | Cross-check with other listings in the area |
| Landlord refuses a video call | Always request a live video walkthrough |
| Wants wire transfer, crypto, or cash upfront | Use secure payment methods (credit card, platform payment system) |
| No written lease | Always get a signed rental agreement |
| Cannot provide a WiFi speed test | Ask for an Ookla speed test result before booking |
| Only communicates via text or email | Insist on a phone or video call |
| Same photos appear in other cities | Reverse image search listing photos |

---

## The smart booking strategy

1. Check fee-free platforms first. Houfy, Furnished Finder, or direct host websites save 14 to 16% on service fees compared to Airbnb.
2. Always ask for the WiFi speed test. Any serious host who caters to remote workers will provide an Ookla result. If they cannot or will not, assume the WiFi is unreliable.
3. Ask about the workspace. "Is there a proper desk and an ergonomic chair?" saves a month of back pain. Nomad Stays and Roampads verify this for you.
4. Book 2 to 3 days on Airbnb first. Then find a monthly rental once you are on the ground. You save money and avoid committing to a neighborhood you have never seen.
5. Negotiate off-platform when safe. Direct booking gives hosts pricing flexibility. Many offer discounts they do not advertise on Airbnb.
6. Use Airbnb's monthly stay filter if you stick with Airbnb. Stays of 28+ nights automatically get 15 to 25% off through Airbnb's built-in discounts.
7. Consider coliving for your first month in a new city. It costs more than an apartment but eliminates setup stress, provides instant community, and gives you time to find a better long term option.

The platform you book on directly impacts how much you pay. For stays of one month or longer, Airbnb's 14 to 16% service fee adds up fast. I use fee-free platforms, negotiate directly with hosts when I can, and always spend a few days in a neighborhood before committing to a month long rental.
